# Admart Exhibition Video Guide — Prompts & Scripts

> **Goal:** Create booth-ready videos in 2–3 days for next week's exhibition.  
> **Recommended deliverables:** 3 videos (or 1 combined reel).  
> **Fastest path:** AI clips for branding + **screen recording** for the real product demo.

---

## Quick Plan (Do This First)

| Video | Length | Purpose | How to Make |
|-------|--------|---------|-------------|
| **A. Brand Loop** | 15–20 sec | Silent loop on booth TV | AI video model only |
| **B. Problem → Solution** | 45–60 sec | Hook visitors walking by | AI clips + voiceover |
| **C. How It Works** | 90–120 sec | Full step-by-step demo | **Screen record Admart** + light voiceover |

**Exhibition tip:** Booth TVs work best **muted with bold captions**. Always burn in text overlays.

**Aspect ratio:** `16:9` for booth screens · export `1080p` minimum.

**Brand look for every prompt:** dark background, blue-to-violet gradient accents, modern SaaS aesthetic, clean cinematic lighting, no clutter.

---

## Global Settings (Use on Every Generation)

Paste this at the end of every visual prompt:

```
Style: cinematic corporate tech, dark UI aesthetic, blue (#2563eb) and violet (#7c3aed) gradient accents, premium SaaS product feel, shallow depth of field, soft rim lighting, 4K quality, smooth camera motion, no watermark, no distorted text, professional marketing video
Aspect ratio: 16:9
Duration: 5 seconds per clip (stitch in editor)
```

**Negative prompt (if your model supports it):**
```
blurry, low quality, cartoon, cluttered, bright white background, illegible text, distorted faces, watermark, logo artifacts, shaky camera, oversaturated
```

# VIDEO C — Step-by-Step Demo (90–120 sec) ⭐ MOST IMPORTANT

**Do NOT generate this fully with AI.** Record your real Admart app for credibility.

### Recording checklist

1. Clean browser, dark theme, demo account logged in
2. Pre-create a project: **"Summer Campaign"**
3. Pre-write a simple prompt: *"30-second product promo for a smart water bottle, upbeat tone, young fitness audience"*
4. Record at **1920×1080**, 60fps if possible
5. Move mouse slowly, pause 2 sec on each click

### Shot list + voiceover (record these 8 steps)

| Step | Screen action | Voiceover | Duration |
|------|---------------|-----------|----------|
| **1** | Dashboard overview | "Welcome to Admart. Your dashboard shows credits, recent videos, and quick actions — everything in one workspace." | 10s |
| **2** | Click **Create Video** | "Start with Create Video. Choose text-to-video, image-to-video, or a ready-made template." | 10s |
| **3** | Type prompt in wizard | "Step one: describe your idea — hook, audience, and tone. Admart builds the shot plan for you." | 12s |
| **4** | Configure: 9:16 + 1080p + platforms | "Step two: pick aspect ratio, resolution, and target platforms — TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, or Facebook." | 12s |
| **5** | Enhance: voiceover + captions ON | "Step three: add AI voiceover, smart captions, and music with one click." | 10s |
| **6** | Click Generate → Progress page | "Hit generate. Admart renders frames, adds audio, burns captions, and encodes — automatically." | 15s |
| **7** | Result preview → Publish page | "Preview your video, edit titles and hashtags per platform, then publish now or schedule." | 15s |
| **8** | Social accounts + Calendar | "Connect your accounts once per project. Plan your content calendar and track analytics — all inside Admart." | 15s |

# Caption / Subtitle Style for Booth

| Setting | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Font | Bold sans-serif (DM Sans Bold or similar) |
| Size | Large — readable from 3 meters |
| Color | White text, black semi-transparent background bar |
| Position | Lower third |
| Highlight | Key words in blue `#2563eb` or violet `#7c3aed` |

**Always caption these words in color:** `Admart`, `AI`, `Publish`, `Free`
